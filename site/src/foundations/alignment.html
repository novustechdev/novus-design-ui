Alignment & content fit

One short value, one line. Identifiers, names, chips, dates, amounts, button labels, and column headers never break across lines, at any width. Layouts make room, scroll, or truncate; they never squeeze a value into two or three rows. The rule is part of constitution Principle IV and an automated layout audit enforces it on every change.

Wrap only free text

Descriptions, notes, and messages are the only content that wraps. In tables they opt in with .cell--wrap, which keeps a readable minimum width; everywhere else paragraphs wrap as normal text.

Use the width you have

The mirror of the single-line rule: a paragraph should not stack into three or four lines while a quarter of its row sits empty. Portal text runs to the width of its container. A reading measure is a deliberate choice, marked with .measure, and belongs to long-form prose, not to a console screen.

The layout audit flags any portal text block that wraps to three or more lines while a quarter or more of its row stays unused and nothing sits beside it. Text that shares its row with a chart or a card is not flagged: the space is in use.

Truncate fixed slots

operations.administrator@novustech.com.sg

When a value must fit a slot it cannot grow, such as a user name in the header, .truncate keeps it on one line with an ellipsis. The full value must stay available: in title, in the opened menu, or nearby.

Numbers and actions align to the end

  • Amounts, counts, and percentages are right-aligned with tabular numerals (class="num" on the header and the cells), so digits line up.
  • Row actions sit in the last column, aligned to the end (.cell--actions); screen actions sit at the end of the title row in the page header.
  • Text, identifiers, and dates are left-aligned. Never centre data.

One control height per row

Checklist

  • Every data table sits in .tablewrap; only free-text columns carry .cell--wrap.
  • Chips, badges, buttons, breadcrumb items, navigation labels, filter triggers, and footer text are single-line by construction in the console layer.
  • A value that must fit a fixed slot uses .truncate and keeps its full text available.
  • Toolbars keep one control height and one row; on narrow screens they move as whole groups or scroll, never breaking a label.
  • Check at 1440px and 375px. The layout audit (scripts/layout-audit.mjs) fails any page where short content wraps or the page scrolls sideways.
