Rules

  • A photo is a framed figure: hairline border, token radius, explicit box, object-fit:cover. Never stretched, never sizing itself from intrinsic pixels.
  • Imagery is candid, natural-light, predominantly-Asian/mixed, a working operations floor, not stock "glowing globe" tech art.
  • No third-party or client branding in generic imagery; client-branded photos live only in explicit proof sections.
  • Overlay captions sit on a system-blue scrim (--blue-900 at alpha), never an off-system dark.
