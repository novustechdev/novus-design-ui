Actions & placement

Where an action sits tells the user what it does before they read it. These rules make placement predictable across every Novus product: progression anchors right, context anchors left, danger stands apart, and there is only ever one primary.

The rules

  • One primary per view. A single accent-filled button carries the main intent; everything else is secondary or ghost. Two primaries compete and both lose.
  • Forms lead with the primary on the left. At the end of a page-level form, the primary sits first (left-aligned with the fields the eye just travelled down), with secondary actions after it. The eye lands on it naturally at the end of the fill-in path.
  • Dialogs and wizards anchor the primary far-right. In a modal footer or wizard bar, the confirming action sits far-right (the direction of progression), Cancel directly to its left, and Back far-left. Users hit the corner without reading.
  • Destructive actions stand apart. Use the danger variant, place it far-left in a footer (opposite the safe path), and never adjacent to the confirm button: distance prevents misclicks that words cannot.
  • Toolbars: work on the left, view on the right. Bulk and creation actions start the row; filters, density, and export controls end it.

Spacing between actions

  • Actions sit on a .row, which spaces them with var(--space-3): that is the standard button gap.
  • Bare sibling buttons outside a .row still get an automatic var(--space-2) gap from the kit's fallback rule, so buttons never touch, but reach for .row first.
  • Never add ad-hoc pixel margins between buttons; the two gaps above are the whole system.
