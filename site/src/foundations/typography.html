One voice across the shell

In a portal the header, the side navigation and the page content are set in one family at one size. The header is never smaller than the content it frames, and headings stay close to body size so a screen reads as one document. This is a constitution rule (Principle IV) and the gates measure it on every build.

RoleTokenSizeRatio to body
Header, navigation, body, tables--text-sm14px1.00x
Page title--text-xl24px1.71x
Section heading--text-lg20px1.43x
Sub-heading--text-md18px1.29x
Secondary and captions--text-xs12px0.86x
  • Page titles stay within 1.75 times body size, section headings within 1.45 times. Beyond that a screen stops reading as one document.
  • KPI figures are data, not headings: they keep their display size and are exempt from the ratio.
  • Marketing pages are not portals. The landing page keeps the display scale; the rule applies to consoles and the screens inside them.

Rules

  • Weights: 400 regular, 700 bold; 600 falls back to 700. Emphasis by weight, never italics or all-caps shouting.
  • Body copy caps at ~68ch for readability (the kit sets this on paragraphs).
  • Carlito covers Latin plus common punctuation; exotic Unicode symbols render as tofu, draw icons as inline SVG instead.
  • Decks use a separate, larger slide scale (tokens.css §9), never on web pages.
