Usage

  • Tabs are native radio inputs switched by CSS :has(): no JavaScript, keyboard arrows work, and where :has is unsupported the panels degrade to stacking, never disappearing.
  • The animated accent underline is the active indicator; hover shows a background tint. Both are functional motion, 0.2s or less, disabled under reduced-motion.
  • Tabs divide one subject into facets. For moving between subjects use navigation; for a linear flow use the stepper.
  • Keep labels to one or two words; five tabs at most.
