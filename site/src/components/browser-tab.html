Usage

  • Every icon slot gets its own file, cut from the 512 master at the size the slot draws: 16 and 32 for browser tabs, 180 for the apple-touch-icon, 512 for the manifest. A tab slot is 16px; handing it a 3 kB master makes the browser scale it and still look soft.
  • The tab title is the product name, Novus Design Kit, and nothing else. It does not follow the menu or the page, so a row of open pages stays one recognizable name.
  • The 512 master serves the manifest only — installed-app launchers are the one place a large icon is wanted. Head, verbatim:
<title>Novus Design Kit</title>
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="32x32" href="/assets/favicon-32.png">
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="16x16" href="/assets/favicon-16.png">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" sizes="180x180" href="/assets/favicon-180.png">
