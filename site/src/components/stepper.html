Usage

  • The stepper is composed from shipped primitives: numbered .badge markers on a .row, connectors drawn with a token border.
  • The current step is the only accent element (.badge--accent plus bold label) and carries aria-current="step"; completed steps show a check, upcoming steps stay neutral.
  • Use an ordered list so the sequence reads correctly to assistive technology.
  • Keep it to five steps or fewer; longer flows should collapse into stages.
