Usage

  • The calendar is a presentation pattern composed from the shipped .table: week rows, muted days from adjacent months, the highlighted day as an accent badge.
  • Use it to display schedules and availability; for date entry, use the Date picker (the native control) instead.
  • One accent per view: the highlighted day is the only accent-colored element in the grid.
  • Month navigation is a pair of ghost buttons on the header row; wire them to your data layer.
