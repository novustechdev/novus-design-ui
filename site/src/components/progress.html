Usage

  • Track is --bg-muted, fill is the accent; a judgement colour replaces the accent only when the bar expresses a judgement (a closing deadline, a failing quota).
  • Always pair the bar with a text value; the bar alone is not readable enough for operations work.
  • Use role="progressbar" with the aria value attributes, or a native <progress> element where its styling limits are acceptable.
  • For indeterminate waits use the kit's spinner, not a looping bar.
