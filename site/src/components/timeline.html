Usage

  • An ordered list with a token border as the spine; dots are judgement-coloured only where a judgement exists (completed green, risk red), otherwise a neutral outline.
  • Newest first for operational feeds; oldest first for case histories that read as a story.
  • Every entry carries an absolute timestamp; relative times ("2 hours ago") drift and are banned in audit views.
  • Keep entries to two lines: what happened, then when and the key figure.
