Usage

  • Charts are drawn with D3 (the kit's charting standard) through novus-chart.js: volumeChart for composition plus trajectory, donutChart for share. See them live in the Admin Kit demos.
  • Every colour and font is read from the tokens at render time, so a theme change redraws the chart correctly and no colour literal ever enters the code.
  • The locked rules: soft dashed gridlines at low alpha, rounded bars with constrained thickness, no axis borders or tick marks, a dark rounded tooltip, one composition read and one trajectory read on the primary series.
  • Give the chart a .chartframe to size it and a table of the same numbers beside or under it. The table is the answer for people without scripting, and for anyone who wants the figures.
  • Marks answer hover and keyboard focus with the same tooltip; series colours are the product accents, never traffic-light colours, because volume is data and not a judgement.
