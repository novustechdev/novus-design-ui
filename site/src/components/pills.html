Usage

  • A pill is a chip that does something; a badge is a chip that says something. Labels and states are badges; tags, filters, and selections are pills.
  • Toggle filters with aria-pressed, not a colour swap alone — the selected pill gets the accent tint and the state is announced.
  • Removable pills carry a .pill__x button with its own aria-label naming what it removes; "Remove" alone reads as noise to a screen reader.
  • Counts live inside a pill as a .badge, judgement colour only when the number judges.
  • Lay pills out in a .row so they wrap instead of colliding on a phone.
