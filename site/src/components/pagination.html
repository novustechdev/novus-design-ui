Usage

  • Every paged data list ends with the list footer: the range at the start; previous, the page label, next, and rows per page at the end.
  • Rows per page offers 10, 20, and 50, defaulting to 10. Changing it returns to page 1.
  • Previous is disabled on the first page and next on the last; both are 44px icon buttons with names.
  • The range always states the total: "Showing 21-24 of 24" on a short last page, "Showing 0 of 0" when nothing matches.
  • Numbered pages suit long documents and search results where jumping matters; the current page carries the accent border and aria-current="page".
