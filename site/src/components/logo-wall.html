Usage

  • Marks keep their original full colour on white tiles, never recolour, invert, or CSS-filter a client or partner mark. Tiles stay white in dark mode.
  • Logo tiles are non-clickable, so they get no hover response.
  • The production client wall shows all 13 canonical client marks, never a hand-picked subset. This demo truncates for space only.
  • Client tiles run larger than scheme tiles so both read at equal optical weight.
