Usage

  • Search takes the remaining width; the Filters trigger sits at the end of the same row and shows how many filters are on.
  • The sheet has two panes: categories on one side (native radio tabs), the chosen category's options on the other (checkboxes). Choosing a category never closes the sheet; long option lists scroll inside their pane; six categories at most. On phones the categories sit above the options.
  • Every ticked option appears as a removable chip naming its category and value, followed by Clear all. Options within a category combine with OR; categories combine with AND.
  • Quick chips with live counts sit above queue-style lists for the one dimension people switch most (status, bucket); exactly one is selected. Keep that dimension out of the sheet so the two controls never disagree.
  • Only a few filters? Use the compact row: plain controls on one line that scrolls sideways instead of wrapping.
  • Everything opens and closes with JavaScript off (native details, radios, checkboxes); applying the filters is the application's job, and an empty result offers to clear them.
