Usage

  • An ordered list in a labelled nav; the current page is plain text with aria-current="page", never a link.
  • The separator is the kit's › glyph (the same one the bullets component uses), muted and hidden from assistive technology.
  • Three levels is the comfortable maximum; collapse deeper paths from the middle.
  • Breadcrumbs state location; they do not replace the section navigation.
