Usage

  • A slide-over is a <dialog class="slideover">. The right edge pin, focus trap, Esc, and the inert page behind come from the element itself; showModal() opens it, nothing else does.
  • Give the dialog an aria-labelledby pointing at its title so a screen reader announces the record, not just "dialog".
  • Use it when the task stays on one record and the page behind is context worth keeping visible. A modal asks for a decision; a slide-over shows a record and offers actions at its foot.
  • The slide is a keyframe on [open], not a measured transform. Nothing scripts the panel open or shut, so it costs nothing and cannot desync.
  • Body scrolls on its own and carries overscroll-behavior: contain, so scrolling to the foot does not drag the page behind it. On a phone the panel goes full width; past 60rem the body splits into content and a details column.
  • Counts and attachments live in the details column as a <dl class="slideover__facts">: muted term left, value right, and anything countable gets a .badge chip so a growing queue is visible at a glance.
  • Actions sit right in .slideover__foot with the primary last. Keep them to two: a cancel and the one thing the panel exists to do.
